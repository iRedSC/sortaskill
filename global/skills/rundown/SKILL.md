# Rundown

Inspect only enough to ground every claim. For a planned change, trace the primary path, nearby callers, and relevant tests.

Deliver everything critical the user needs to know as separate bite-sized pieces of 2-4 sentences each. Bold the most important sentence in each piece. Do not open a piece with a label or title. Cover the problem or current state, what you'd do, and any risk or open question that would change their decision. Drop anything that wouldn't.

Do not turn it into a full implementation plan.
