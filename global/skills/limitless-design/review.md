# Review

Read this only after the designs are built. Screenshot each design top to bottom at desktop and mobile widths, then check it against both lists. Rework anything that fails; do not just restyle it.

## Does it belong?

- Every section and element belongs to the theme's world. A plain table, card grid, or label row below a themed centerpiece means the theme stopped early.
- The page is not the usual outline with a themed widget dropped in. Nav, headline, features, pricing, and footer each take a form from the theme.
- The first screen differs in composition from the other designs, not only in color and type.
- Every interaction is finished: each state it can reach is designed, and nothing is misaligned, clipped, or empty.
- Any motion works as a sequence, not only at the moment you happened to screenshot.

## Generated-page habits

These appear regardless of subject, so they read as defaults rather than choices. Keep one only if the theme truly calls for it.

- Warm cream background with a high-contrast serif display and a terracotta or clay accent.
- Near-black background with a single acid-green, vermilion, or yellow accent.
- One word or phrase in the headline set apart in italic, bold, or an accent color.
- Small all-caps tracked labels above headings, and labels in general that say nothing new.
- Meta strings joined with middle dots, like `Field notes · Plate I`.
- Numbered markers (`01`, `i.`) on content that is not a real sequence.
- Content chopped into identical rounded cards with the same soft shadow.
- A three-column feature row, a lone testimonial, and two side-by-side pricing cards.
- Nav of "How it works, Pricing, Sign in" and a footer of "Privacy, Terms, Contact".
- Monospace for small data labels, and an arrow appended to button text.
- Fade-and-slide-up on every section, hover lifts on every card, and parallax for its own sake.
