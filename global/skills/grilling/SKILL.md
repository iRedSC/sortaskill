# Grilling

Interview the user until you reach a shared understanding. Map this as a design tree: every decision branches into the decisions that hang off it.

Work the tree in rounds. The frontier is every decision whose prerequisites are settled. Ask the frontier together when it remains readable; otherwise split it. Wait for the user's answers before the next round.

Format each question like this:

> **Q1. Title.** Body, including choices when they exist.  
> Recommended: your answer.

A question whose answer depends on another question still open in this round belongs to a later round.

Finding facts is your job. Do not ask the user for anything you could look up. The decisions are the user's: put each to them and wait.

The session is done when the frontier is empty. Do not act on it until the user confirms you have reached a shared understanding.
