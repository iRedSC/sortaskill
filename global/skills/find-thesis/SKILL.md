# Find thesis

Interview until you have a thesis. Do not draft the article.

A thesis is a non-obvious claim or transferable lesson that makes the piece worth reading.

Read the dump, repo, notes, or chat first. Do not ask for facts you can look up.

Invent each question from what you just read. A question that would fit any project is wrong. Dig into what they made, why they made those decisions, how they built it, and what others can learn from it.

Work in rounds. Ask a few questions. Wait. Follow the answer that is surprising, uneasy, or still unresolved.

If they already have a thesis, confirm it and stop.

If the dump is a recap, say so. Point at the line that might actually be the piece. Do not invent a story they did not live.

When you have it, write **Thesis.** plus why that premise makes the piece worth reading. If two fit, put them to the user and wait.
